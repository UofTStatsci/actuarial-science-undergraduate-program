University of Toronto / Actuarial Science

The science
of us.

Actuarial Science uses mathematics, statistics and computational methods to understand and manage risk. From insurance and pensions to investment and financial security, actuaries help organizations make informed decisions about the future.

U of T offers one of the best Actuarial Science programs in North America. With courses dating back to 1875, it is the oldest actuarial science program on the continent.

01 / Actuarial Science Specialist

Learn the theory.
Apply the practice.

The Specialist program combines actuarial theory courses with five practicum courses covering the major subfields of actuarial science and professional communication.

Courses are taught by senior practising actuaries, giving students practical knowledge across different professional practice areas.

02 / Actuarial Science Major

Mathematical thinking.
Real-world impact.

The Major program offers students a foundation in actuarial science, including the mathematical, statistical and financial methods used to evaluate and manage uncertainty.

Students develop an understanding of how actuarial methods are used across insurance, pensions, finance and other industries.

Internships and careers

A career built
around possibility.

Actuarial Science applies mathematical techniques to reduce the impact of risk, from loss of income resulting from death, disability or retirement to the loss of property through fire, accidents or theft.

Actuaries design life, health, property and pension insurance plans, and help ensure their financial soundness.

01

Insurance and reinsurance

Life and annuities, healthcare, property and casualty, reinsurance and actuarial consulting.

02

Pensions and investment

Pension plan valuation, investment management, quantitative finance and financial risk.

03

Government and emerging fields

Government departments, social insurance, actuarial software, climate risk management and microinsurance.

Employment outlook

Employment of actuaries is projected to grow by 9% between 2025 and 2035, much faster than the average for all occupations.

Where your education can take you

Graduates apply mathematical, statistical and machine learning methods across financial, insurance, public-sector and emerging risk management industries.

Professional recognition

Your pathway
to the profession.

The Actuarial Science program is fully accredited under the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (CIA) University Accreditation Program.

U of T is one of only 12 programs in Canada to receive full accreditation.

Associate of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries

Students completing the program are eligible for the CIA capstone examination, which leads to the professional credential of Associate of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (ACIA) without having to take external professional exams.

Many courses are taught by senior, fully credentialed practicing actuaries, providing students with direct exposure to professional practice.
